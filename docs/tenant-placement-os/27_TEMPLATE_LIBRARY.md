>
> Once that's done, I'll run a market pricing analysis, get the property rent-ready if needed, and move to marketing and launch. You'll hear from me at every major step — you'll never have to chase me for an update.

## What Happens Next Email
**Subject: [Address] — the process, start to finish**

> Quick overview of every stage so you know what's coming:
>
> 1. **Pricing** — I analyze the market and recommend a launch range
> 2. **Preparation** — property gets rent-ready and marketing assets built
> 3. **Launch** — listed everywhere relevant, vacancy clock starts
> 4. **Leasing** — leads, showings, and applications managed daily
> 5. **Screening** — every applicant verified consistently
> 6. **Your decision** — I bring you a verified applicant summary, you decide
> 7. **Lease + move-in** — signed, deposited, handed off cleanly
>
> You'll get updates at launch, day 7, day 14, and any time something needs your input. Questions any time — just reply here.

## Vacancy Cost Calculator
```
Daily vacancy cost = (monthly rent × 12) ÷ 365
Estimated loss so far = daily vacancy cost × days vacant
```
Use this number directly in the Price Reduction Conversation Script (`25_SCRIPT_LIBRARY.md`) — never present a price change recommendation without it.
