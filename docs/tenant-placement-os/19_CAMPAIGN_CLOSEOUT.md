# 19 — Campaign Closeout

## PURPOSE
Capture the complete record of how this placement actually went, in numbers, before memory fades. This is the raw material for `20_POSTMORTEM.md` and `22_MANAGEMENT_SCORECARD.md`. Serves: **Money**, **Vacancy→Tenant**.

## WHEN THIS SOP STARTS
Immediately after `18_MOVE_IN_HANDOFF.md` is complete.

## RESPONSIBLE PERSON
Leasing Lead.

## ESTIMATED TIME
15 minutes.

## REQUIRED INPUTS
- Full campaign history: launch record, funnel scoreboard, all price changes, all owner decisions logged

---

## CLOSEOUT RECORD — **[CHECKLIST]** for all fields below

- [ ] Original asking rent (from `08_LISTING_LAUNCH.md` launch record)
- [ ] Final asking rent (if changed during the campaign)
- [ ] Final leased rent
- [ ] Days to lease (Day 0 to lease signature date)
- [ ] Total inquiries
- [ ] Total showings
- [ ] Total applications
- [ ] Total qualified applications
- [ ] Lead source breakdown (which channels produced inquiries)
- [ ] Winning lead source (which channel produced the leased tenant)
- [ ] Price changes made, with dates and reasons
- [ ] Incentives offered
- [ ] Top objection reasons across all showings (from `10_SHOWINGS.md` logs)
- [ ] Owner decisions made during the campaign (from the Owner Decision Log, `27_TEMPLATE_LIBRARY.md`)

- [ ] Compile into the Campaign Closeout Template — **[AUTOMATABLE]** where data is already tracked, **[CHECKLIST]** to compile
  DEFINITION OF DONE: a single record a stranger could read to understand exactly how this placement went, start to finish

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Waiting weeks to compile this — do it immediately after handoff while the funnel data and reasoning are still accurate and complete.
- Leaving "top objection reasons" blank because it requires pulling from multiple showing logs — this is the most valuable field for `20_POSTMORTEM.md` and shouldn't be skipped for convenience.

## ESCALATION CONDITIONS
- None — this is a data-compilation step, not a decision point.

## QUALITY CONTROL
- Every closed placement has a complete Campaign Closeout record before `20_POSTMORTEM.md` begins.

## OUTPUT / DELIVERABLE
A complete Campaign Closeout record feeding directly into the postmortem and the management scorecard.
