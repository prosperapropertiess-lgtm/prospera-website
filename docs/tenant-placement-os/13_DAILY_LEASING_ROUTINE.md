# 13 — Daily Leasing Routine

## PURPOSE
Give the person running leasing one fixed, repeatable 20-minute routine that always answers "what needs attention today?" — so nothing depends on remembering to check something. Serves: **Vacancy→Tenant**, **Owner Communication**.

## WHEN THIS SOP STARTS
Every business day, same time (recommended: first thing in the morning, before other work).

## RESPONSIBLE PERSON
Leasing Lead.

## ESTIMATED TIME
20 minutes.

## REQUIRED INPUTS
- Access to lead pipeline, active vacancy list, showing calendar

---

## THE 20-MINUTE ROUTINE

- [ ] **New leads (3 min)** — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Every lead with STATUS=NEW gets first contact today, within the response-time target in `09_LEAD_MANAGEMENT.md`.

- [ ] **Warm leads (4 min)** — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Every CONTACTED/QUALIFIED lead — is NEXT ACTION due today? Handle it now.

- [ ] **Showings (3 min)** — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Confirm today's and tomorrow's showings are still on. Any completed showing from yesterday missing an outcome? Log it now.

- [ ] **Applications (3 min)** — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Any application stalled at Documents Missing or Reference Outstanding for more than 3 business days? Send the chase script.

- [ ] **Follow-ups (3 min)** — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Any lead due for a no-response follow-up (24h/72h/7-day) today? Send it.

- [ ] **Overdue tasks (2 min)** — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Any lead with a blank NEXT ACTION or a NEXT ACTION DATE in the past? This should be zero — if not, fix immediately.

- [ ] **Funnel performance (2 min)** — **[CHECKLIST]**
  For each active vacancy, glance at the scoreboard (`22_MANAGEMENT_SCORECARD.md` / `14_FUNNEL_DIAGNOSTICS.md`) — is anything trending wrong before it hits a formal escalation checkpoint?

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Skipping the routine on a "quiet" day — quiet days are exactly when a stalled lead goes unnoticed longest.
- Doing the routine out of order and running out of time before reaching overdue tasks — the order above is intentional, follow it.
- Treating this as optional once volume is low — the routine matters more, not less, when there's only 1–2 active vacancies, because every lead counts more.

## ESCALATION CONDITIONS
- Overdue tasks found 2+ days in a row → signal that either volume has exceeded capacity to manage, or the routine itself is being skipped — flag to Ebin.

## QUALITY CONTROL
- Routine completed every business day, logged with a timestamp (even a simple "done" checkmark).

## OUTPUT / DELIVERABLE
A daily confirmation that no lead, showing, application, or follow-up is stuck without a next step.
