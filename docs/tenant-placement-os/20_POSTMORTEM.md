# 20 — Postmortem

## PURPOSE
Turn every placement into a lesson, on purpose, within days — not accumulated hindsight nobody ever reviews. Serves: all 5 pillars indirectly, by making the whole system get better over time.

## WHEN THIS SOP STARTS
Within 48 hours of lease signing.

## RESPONSIBLE PERSON
Leasing Lead (with Ebin for any placement that had a major deviation from plan).

## ESTIMATED TIME
10 minutes.

## REQUIRED INPUTS
- Campaign Closeout record (`19_CAMPAIGN_CLOSEOUT.md`)
- Original rent recommendation and confidence level (`04_RENT_PRICING.md`)

---

## POSTMORTEM QUESTIONS — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]** for all

- [ ] What did we predict? (Original rent recommendation, confidence level, expected timeline if one was informally expected)
- [ ] What actually happened? (Final rent, actual days to lease)
- [ ] Where did the funnel break, if anywhere? (Weakest conversion point per `14_FUNNEL_DIAGNOSTICS.md`)
- [ ] What intervention worked?
- [ ] What didn't work?
- [ ] Was the original price correct? (Compare original asking vs. final leased rent, and whether a price change was needed)
- [ ] What objections repeated? (Top objection reasons from closeout)
- [ ] Did owner decisions delay leasing? (Any point where an owner decision, or delay in one, cost time)
- [ ] Did Prospera make mistakes? (Be specific and honest — this connects to `16_OWNER_COMMUNICATION.md`'s "when we get something wrong" pattern if anything here wasn't already surfaced to the owner at the time)
- [ ] What should happen earlier next time?

- [ ] Log answers using the Postmortem Template — **[CHECKLIST]**
  TEMPLATE: `27_TEMPLATE_LIBRARY.md`

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Turning this into a defense of decisions made instead of an honest look — the value is entirely in honesty.
- Skipping postmortems on placements that went smoothly — smooth placements often reveal what's actually working and worth repeating deliberately.
- Letting more than 48 hours pass — details fade fast.

## ESCALATION CONDITIONS
- A postmortem reveals a repeated pattern across multiple placements (e.g., pricing consistently starts too high) → raise as an agenda item at the next Management Scorecard review (`22_MANAGEMENT_SCORECARD.md`), don't let it just live in one file.

## QUALITY CONTROL
- Every closed placement has a completed postmortem within 48 hours.

## OUTPUT / DELIVERABLE
A completed Postmortem record per placement, feeding lessons back into the relevant SOP (pricing, funnel, communication) when a pattern repeats.
