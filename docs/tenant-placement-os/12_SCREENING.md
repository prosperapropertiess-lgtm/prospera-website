# 12 — Screening SOP

## PURPOSE
Verify applicants consistently and lawfully, every time, regardless of who's running the process. Serves: **Vacancy→Tenant**, **Money**.

## WHEN THIS SOP STARTS
An applicant completes Step 3 (Document/Verification Request) in `11_APPLICATIONS.md`.

## RESPONSIBLE PERSON
Leasing Lead.

## ESTIMATED TIME
30–60 minutes per applicant, spread across reference/employer response times (typically 2–5 business days end to end).

## REQUIRED INPUTS
- Submitted documents (ID, proof of income/employment, rental history/references)
- Credit information, where lawfully obtained and consented to

---

## ⚠️ [LEGAL REVIEW NEEDED] — READ BEFORE USING THIS SOP
This checklist describes an operational verification process. It does **not** state specific Ontario legal thresholds (what can lawfully be required as a condition of tenancy, permissible use of credit checks, deposit rules, or exact Human Rights Code case law). Have this section reviewed by someone qualified in Ontario landlord-tenant and human rights law before treating any line below as a compliance guarantee. Nothing here should be read as legal advice.

## NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE
**Never screen, question, or decide based on a protected ground under the Ontario Human Rights Code** — including but not limited to family status, disability, receipt of public assistance, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, marital status, ethnic origin, place of origin, race, ancestry, or age. This mirrors the standing rule already in this repo's `COPYWRITING.md` ("never describe the ideal tenant... Ontario Human Rights Code") and applies with the same force here. If a screening criterion cannot be explained purely in terms of ability to pay and meet lease obligations, it does not belong in this process.

## SCREENING CHECKLIST

- [ ] Identity verification — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Government-issued photo ID matches the applicant's name on the application.

- [ ] Income/employment verification — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**, **[CHECKLIST]** for document collection
  SCRIPT: Employment Verification Script (`25_SCRIPT_LIBRARY.md`) — verify directly with employer/HR where possible, not just from a pay stub alone.

- [ ] Rental history verification — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
  SCRIPT: Landlord Reference Script — same questions asked consistently for every applicant: payment history, notice given at move-out, any lease violations, would they rent to this person again.

- [ ] Reference checks — **[CHECKLIST]** for tracking, **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]** for the calls themselves

- [ ] Credit information, where lawfully obtained — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Only with the applicant's informed consent. **[LEGAL REVIEW NEEDED]** on consent language and permissible use.

- [ ] Document consistency check — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
  Names, dates, employer details consistent across all submitted documents — the most common early fraud indicator is small inconsistencies, not obviously fake documents.

- [ ] Fraud indicator review — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
  Red flags: pay stubs that don't match a real, verifiable employer; references that don't answer as a real landlord would; urgency/pressure to skip verification steps.

- [ ] Compile Owner Applicant Summary — **[AI ASSISTABLE]** for drafting, **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]** for review
  TEMPLATE: `27_TEMPLATE_LIBRARY.md` — present findings as facts, not a recommendation dressed as a decision; the owner decides.

## RED FLAG ESCALATION PROCEDURE
- [ ] Any fraud indicator found → pause the application, do not proceed to owner presentation until resolved — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
- [ ] Escalate to Ebin directly for any suspected document fraud — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
- [ ] Document exactly what triggered the flag, in neutral factual language — **[CHECKLIST]**

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Asking different applicants different questions — consistency is the entire point; inconsistent screening is both unfair and a legal exposure.
- Treating a screening criterion as reasonable because "it's just common sense" without checking it against the Human Rights Code protected-grounds list above.
- Skipping direct employer verification because a pay stub "looks fine."

## ESCALATION CONDITIONS
- Any applicant screening question or criterion whose lawfulness is unclear → do not use it, flag **[LEGAL REVIEW NEEDED]**, escalate to Ebin before applying it to any applicant.

## QUALITY CONTROL
- Same verification checklist applied to every applicant, no exceptions based on how the conversation "felt."
- No decision made or implied based on a protected ground.

## OUTPUT / DELIVERABLE
An Owner Applicant Summary presenting verified facts (identity, income, rental history, references, fraud check) for the owner's decision.
