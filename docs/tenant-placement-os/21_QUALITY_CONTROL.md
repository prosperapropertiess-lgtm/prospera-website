# 21 — Quality Control Standards

## PURPOSE
State the non-negotiables in one place, independent of any single SOP, so quality is checkable at a glance rather than inferred from reading 20 separate files. Serves: all 5 pillars.

## WHEN THIS SOP STARTS
Continuous — referenced during the Daily Leasing Routine and any audit.

## RESPONSIBLE PERSON
Leasing Lead; Ebin for periodic audits.

## ESTIMATED TIME
Ongoing.

## REQUIRED INPUTS
- Access to lead pipeline, active vacancies, owner communication logs

---

## NON-NEGOTIABLE SERVICE STANDARDS

- [ ] No legitimate inquiry ignored. (`09_LEAD_MANAGEMENT.md`)
- [ ] No active lead without a NEXT ACTION and NEXT ACTION DATE. (`09_LEAD_MANAGEMENT.md`, `13_DAILY_LEASING_ROUTINE.md`)
- [ ] No showing without a logged outcome. (`10_SHOWINGS.md`)
- [ ] No owner without a scheduled next update. (`16_OWNER_COMMUNICATION.md`)
- [ ] No price recommendation without documented evidence (comp set + confidence level). (`04_RENT_PRICING.md`)
- [ ] No applicant left waiting unnecessarily — status update at minimum every 3 business days while active. (`11_APPLICATIONS.md`)
- [ ] No property accepted without a capacity check. (`02_CAPACITY_MANAGEMENT.md`)
- [ ] No vacancy allowed to sit without diagnosis — escalation checkpoints are maximums, act on data earlier when it's there. (`15_VACANCY_ESCALATION.md`)
- [ ] No screening decision based on a protected ground under the Ontario Human Rights Code. (`12_SCREENING.md`) **[LEGAL REVIEW NEEDED]**
- [ ] No BLOCKER-classified rent-ready item unresolved at listing launch. (`06_RENT_READY.md`)
- [ ] No lease signed that deviates from owner-authorized terms without a logged owner approval. (`17_LEASE_EXECUTION.md`)
- [ ] No handoff item skipped at move-in. (`18_MOVE_IN_HANDOFF.md`)

## AUDIT CHECKLIST (run monthly, alongside the CEO Dashboard sit-down)

- [ ] Spot-check 3 random active/recent leads against all 12 standards — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
- [ ] Note any standard that failed and why — **[CHECKLIST]**
- [ ] If a standard fails more than once across the sample, treat it as a process gap, not an individual mistake — trace back to the responsible SOP and revise it — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Treating these as aspirational instead of literal pass/fail checks.
- Auditing only when something has already gone wrong, instead of on a fixed monthly cadence.

## ESCALATION CONDITIONS
- Any standard failing in the audit sample twice in a row → revise the underlying SOP, don't just note it and move on.

## QUALITY CONTROL
- This file *is* the quality control layer for the whole system — it has no higher-level check above it except the monthly audit.

## OUTPUT / DELIVERABLE
A monthly audit result: which standards held, which didn't, and what SOP change (if any) was made in response.
