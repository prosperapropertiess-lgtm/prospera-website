# 03 — Pre-Signing Expectation Setting

## PURPOSE
Make sure the owner understands what tenant placement actually is — a professional process with real controllables, not a guarantee — before any agreement is signed. This prevents the single most common source of mid-vacancy conflict: an owner who believed something was promised that never was. Serves: **Owner Communication**, **Vacancy→Tenant**.

## WHEN THIS SOP STARTS
Immediately after a property passes `01_PROPERTY_ACCEPTANCE.md` (Green or conditionally-accepted Yellow), before any agreement is sent for signature.

## RESPONSIBLE PERSON
Leasing Lead.

## ESTIMATED TIME
10–15 minutes, live (call or in person) — not an email alone.

## REQUIRED INPUTS
- Accepted leaseability score/band
- Any conditions attached to a Yellow acceptance
- Owner's desired rent and move-in timeline

---

## WHAT THE OWNER MUST UNDERSTAND BEFORE SIGNING

**Prospera controls:** pricing advice, market analysis, presentation, listing quality, distribution, response speed, follow-up, showings, application process, screening process, communication, data collection, recommendations, execution.

**Prospera does not control:** market demand, whether a specific renter likes the property, the owner's final pricing decision, economic conditions, competitor supply.

## EXPECTATION-SETTING CHECKLIST

- [ ] Explain the process is a pipeline, not an event — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
  WHO: Leasing Lead · SCRIPT: Tenant Placement Expectations Script (`25_SCRIPT_LIBRARY.md`) · DEFINITION OF DONE: owner can repeat back, in their own words, that the process has stages · NEXT: cover pricing

- [ ] Set expectations on pricing — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
  Owner must hear, explicitly: "The rent number we recommend is an estimate, not a guarantee. Final achievable rent depends on how the market actually responds. You make the final pricing call — we'll always tell you what we're seeing and recommend accordingly."
  DEFINITION OF DONE: owner acknowledges rent is a range, not a promise

- [ ] Set expectations on timeline — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
  "Vacancy duration varies. We don't have a fixed number of days we can promise, but we will tell you exactly what's happening at every checkpoint and act early if the funnel says something's wrong." Reference the escalation checkpoints in `15_VACANCY_ESCALATION.md` by name so the owner knows checkpoints exist.

- [ ] Set expectations on screening — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
  "We will not lower our screening standards just to fill the vacancy faster. If a strong applicant takes a bit longer to find, that's the trade we're making on purpose."

- [ ] Confirm communication cadence — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
  State the update schedule from `16_OWNER_COMMUNICATION.md` (Launch, Day 7, Day 14, and as-needed) so the owner never has to ask "any updates?"

- [ ] Confirm escalation path — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
  Tell the owner explicitly what happens if things aren't working (price review, strategy review) and at what points, referencing `15_VACANCY_ESCALATION.md`.

- [ ] Log that expectations were set — **[CHECKLIST]**
  WHO: Leasing Lead · HOW: note on the owner record · REQUIRED DATA: date, method (call/in-person), confirmation the 5 points above were covered · DEFINITION OF DONE: entry exists before the agreement is sent · NEXT: send agreement (`05_OWNER_ONBOARDING.md`)

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Doing this over text or a single email instead of live conversation — expectations set in a one-way message don't stick and can't be confirmed understood.
- Skipping this step for "easy" owners who seem to already get it — every owner gets this conversation, no exceptions, because "I thought you meant..." always shows up with the owner who seemed most low-maintenance.
- Promising a specific lease date to close the deal — never do this. Ranges and honest process, always.

## ESCALATION CONDITIONS
- If an owner pushes back hard on any of the 5 expectation points (e.g., insists on a guaranteed rent number or a guaranteed date), that's itself a signal — flag to Ebin before proceeding, this may indicate the assignment should be reconsidered even if it scored Green.

## QUALITY CONTROL
- No agreement is sent without a logged expectation-setting conversation.

## OUTPUT / DELIVERABLE
A logged confirmation that the owner heard and acknowledged all 5 expectation points, dated before the agreement was sent.
