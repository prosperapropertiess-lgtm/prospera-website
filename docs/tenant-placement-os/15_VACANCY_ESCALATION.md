# 15 — Vacancy Escalation System

## PURPOSE
Guarantee a vacancy is never left to just sit — checkpoints are maximum waiting periods, not a schedule to relax into. Serves: **Vacancy→Tenant**, **Owner Communication**.

## WHEN THIS SOP STARTS
Day 0 (the moment `08_LISTING_LAUNCH.md` starts the vacancy clock) and runs continuously until leased.

## RESPONSIBLE PERSON
Leasing Lead.

## ESTIMATED TIME
15–30 minutes at each checkpoint.

## REQUIRED INPUTS
- Vacancy clock start date
- Current funnel scoreboard (`14_FUNNEL_DIAGNOSTICS.md`)

---

## ⚠️ CHECKPOINTS ARE MAXIMUMS, NOT TARGETS
If the funnel data clearly shows a problem before its scheduled checkpoint, escalate early. Never wait for a calendar date when the data already has the answer.

## ⚠️ VOLUME-BASED TRIGGERS (not just calendar-based)
Day-checkpoints assume a roughly normal pace of activity. A property in a very active market can blow past several stages' worth of activity in just a few days — a calendar-only system would miss that. These triggers fire regardless of what day it is:
- **10+ showings completed with zero applications** → treat as an immediate Day-14-equivalent Market/Pricing Review, don't wait for the calendar date.
- **20+ qualified inquiries with zero showings booked** → treat as an immediate Level 1 Intervention (booking-flow friction, not a pricing problem).
- **5+ qualified applications with zero passing screening** → don't just keep processing more applicants; pause and review screening criteria consistency per `12_SCREENING.md` before continuing (see also Scenario G in `00_MASTER_TENANT_PLACEMENT_WORKFLOW.md`).

## ESCALATION CHECKLIST

- [ ] **Day 3 — 72-Hour Diagnostic** — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Confirm listing is live everywhere, confirm at least some views/inquiries are registering. If zero activity by Day 3, treat as a LOW VIEWS diagnosis immediately (`14_FUNNEL_DIAGNOSTICS.md`) — don't wait for Day 7.

- [ ] **Day 7 — Owner Report** — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
  Send the Day 7 Update (`16_OWNER_COMMUNICATION.md`) regardless of how things are going — numbers, feedback, actions taken, assessment, next update date.

- [ ] **Day 10 — Level 1 Intervention** — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
  Run full funnel diagnostics, apply the specific fix indicated by the weakest conversion point. This is the first "act" checkpoint, not just a check-in.

- [ ] **Day 14 — Market/Pricing Review** — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**, **[OWNER DECISION]** on any price change
  Re-run `04_RENT_PRICING.md` against updated market data. If price is the diagnosed problem, present the Price Reduction Conversation Script (`25_SCRIPT_LIBRARY.md`) with the vacancy cost math already calculated.

- [ ] **Day 21 — Major Strategy Review** — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
  If two corrective actions have already been tried (Day 10, Day 14) without improvement, this is a full reset of assumptions — reconsider photos, copy, channels, price, and property presentation together, not one variable at a time.

- [ ] **Day 30 — Full Reset / Owner Strategy Meeting** — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**, **[OWNER DECISION]**
  A live conversation, not an email. Present full funnel history, everything tried, and 2–3 concrete path-forward options (price change, incentive, property improvement, or — rarely — releasing the assignment). Use the 30-Day Vacancy Review template (`16_OWNER_COMMUNICATION.md` / `27_TEMPLATE_LIBRARY.md`).

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Treating Day 7/14/21/30 as the only times to look at the funnel — the Daily Leasing Routine and Funnel Diagnostics run continuously; these are the guaranteed floor, not the whole system.
- Making a price change at Day 14 without recalculating vacancy economics first (`04_RENT_PRICING.md`, vacancy cost calculator in `27_TEMPLATE_LIBRARY.md`) — the owner needs the tradeoff shown, not just a suggestion.
- Letting Day 30 arrive without ever having escalated early despite clear data earlier — early escalation is a requirement, not a nice-to-have.

## ESCALATION CONDITIONS
- This entire file *is* the escalation system — the meta-escalation rule is: if data says "problem" before the next calendar checkpoint, don't wait.

## QUALITY CONTROL
- Every active vacancy has a checkpoint log showing which day-checkpoints have been completed and what action (if any) was taken at each.

## OUTPUT / DELIVERABLE
A vacancy escalation log per active vacancy, with every checkpoint's outcome and action recorded.
