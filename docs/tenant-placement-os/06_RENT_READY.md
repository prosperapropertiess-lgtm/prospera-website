# 06 — Rent-Ready Inspection

## PURPOSE
Make sure the property is actually showable before a single lead is generated — a great funnel pointed at an unshowable property just wastes leads. Serves: **Vacancy→Tenant**, **Move-In/Move-Out**.

## WHEN THIS SOP STARTS
After `05_OWNER_ONBOARDING.md` is complete, before marketing prep begins.

## RESPONSIBLE PERSON
Leasing Lead (or a designated inspector on more complex properties).

## ESTIMATED TIME
30–60 minutes on-site.

## REQUIRED INPUTS
- Property access (from onboarding)
- Known defects list from onboarding intake

---

## INSPECTION CHECKLIST

Classify every item found as **BLOCKER** (prevents launch), **IMPORTANT** (should be fixed, doesn't block launch), or **COSMETIC** (nice-to-fix, never blocks).

- [ ] Cleanliness — **[CHECKLIST]** — floors, surfaces, appliances, bathroom, no lingering odors
- [ ] Repairs — **[CHECKLIST]** — anything broken, non-functional, or unsafe (checked against onboarding's known-defects list)
- [ ] Paint / walls — **[CHECKLIST]** — holes, major marks, mismatched patches
- [ ] Lighting — **[CHECKLIST]** — every fixture works, bulbs present
- [ ] Appliances — **[CHECKLIST]** — all included appliances power on and function
- [ ] Doors and locks — **[CHECKLIST]** — every door closes/locks properly, keys match
- [ ] Windows — **[CHECKLIST]** — open, close, lock, no cracked glass
- [ ] Bathroom — **[CHECKLIST]** — no leaks, proper water pressure, ventilation working
- [ ] Kitchen — **[CHECKLIST]** — counters, cabinets, plumbing under sink
- [ ] HVAC — **[CHECKLIST]** — heat and A/C both confirmed functional for the season
- [ ] Smells — **[CHECKLIST]** — pet, smoke, mildew — walk in cold, don't get nose-blind
- [ ] Garbage/exterior/yard/snow — **[CHECKLIST]** — curb appeal, seasonal upkeep (snow clearing in winter, lawn in summer)
- [ ] Safety issues — **[CHECKLIST]** — smoke/CO detectors present and working, handrails secure, no trip hazards
- [ ] Showing presentation — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]** — would this photograph and show well right now?

- [ ] Compile the findings list with classifications — **[CHECKLIST]**
  DEFINITION OF DONE: every item is tagged BLOCKER / IMPORTANT / COSMETIC · NEXT: communicate to owner

- [ ] Send required-work communication to owner for any BLOCKER items — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
  SCRIPT: Required Work Communication (`25_SCRIPT_LIBRARY.md`) · DEFINITION OF DONE: owner has a clear list, cost expectation if known, and timeline · NEXT: hold launch until BLOCKERs are resolved

- [ ] Re-inspect after BLOCKER items are addressed — **[CHECKLIST]**
  DEFINITION OF DONE: no remaining BLOCKER items · NEXT: proceed to `07_MARKETING_PREPARATION.md`

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Launching marketing before BLOCKER items are actually resolved because the owner said "it's basically done."
- Confusing IMPORTANT with BLOCKER — IMPORTANT items get flagged and scheduled, they don't stall the vacancy clock.
- Inspecting from memory/owner description instead of walking the property in person.

## ESCALATION CONDITIONS
- Owner refuses to address a genuine safety BLOCKER (no smoke detector, broken lock, etc.) → escalate to Ebin immediately; Prospera should not market a property with an unresolved safety issue. **[LEGAL REVIEW NEEDED]** — confirm Ontario minimum habitability/safety requirements before finalizing what counts as a hard BLOCKER by law versus by Prospera standard.

## QUALITY CONTROL
- No listing goes live with an open BLOCKER item.

## OUTPUT / DELIVERABLE
A classified inspection findings list, owner communication sent for any BLOCKER items, and confirmation of re-inspection if needed.
