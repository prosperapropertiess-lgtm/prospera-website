# 17 — Lease Execution

## PURPOSE
Turn an owner-approved applicant into a signed lease cleanly, with nothing left informal. Serves: **Vacancy→Tenant**, **Money**, **Move-In/Move-Out**.

## WHEN THIS SOP STARTS
The owner approves an applicant (Step 5 of `11_APPLICATIONS.md`).

## RESPONSIBLE PERSON
Leasing Lead.

## ESTIMATED TIME
1–2 hours across document prep, review, and signature.

## REQUIRED INPUTS
- Owner-approved applicant
- Verified screening record (`12_SCREENING.md`)
- Owner's authorized rent/terms from onboarding

---

## ⚠️ [LEGAL REVIEW NEEDED]
Ontario requires use of the standard lease form for most residential tenancies, with specific rules on deposits (last month's rent deposit, not a security/damage deposit) and required disclosures. This checklist describes operational steps only — have lease documents and deposit handling confirmed against current Residential Tenancies Act requirements before this SOP is relied on as compliant.

## CHECKLIST

- [ ] Approval checklist — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Owner approval in writing, applicant still confirmed available/interested, move-in date still workable.

- [ ] Lease preparation checklist — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Correct rent, correct term, correct included utilities/parking, correct names on the lease matching verified ID.

- [ ] Lease review checklist — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
  Full read-through against the property record and owner-authorized terms before sending for signature. **[LEGAL REVIEW NEEDED]** on lease form compliance itself.

- [ ] Signature checklist — **[CHECKLIST]**
  All required parties signed, dated, copies distributed to owner and tenant.

- [ ] Payment/deposit checklist — **[CHECKLIST]**
  **[LEGAL REVIEW NEEDED]** on exact deposit rules — collect only what's lawfully permitted (last month's rent deposit under Ontario rules, not a separate damage deposit), receipt issued.

- [ ] Owner notification — **[AI ASSISTABLE]** draft, **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]** send
  SCRIPT/TEMPLATE: Lease Signed Update (`16_OWNER_COMMUNICATION.md`).

- [ ] Tenant welcome communication — **[AI ASSISTABLE]** draft
  TEMPLATE: `27_TEMPLATE_LIBRARY.md`.

- [ ] Listing removal checklist — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Remove/mark-leased on every channel listed in `08_LISTING_LAUNCH.md`'s launch record — cross-check the full channel list, don't rely on memory.

- [ ] Other applicant communication — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
  Every other active applicant on this property gets a courteous close-out, not silence.

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Removing the listing from only some channels — check the full recorded channel list from `08_LISTING_LAUNCH.md`.
- Leaving other applicants without any closing communication — this damages Prospera's reputation with future applicants.
- Rushing lease review because the owner is eager to close — the review step doesn't get skipped for urgency.

## ESCALATION CONDITIONS
- Any lease term the owner wants that conflicts with what was authorized at onboarding → confirm explicitly with the owner before proceeding, don't assume.

## QUALITY CONTROL
- Every signed lease matches the owner-authorized terms exactly, or has a logged, explicit owner change-approval.

## OUTPUT / DELIVERABLE
A fully signed lease, deposit collected and receipted, listing removed everywhere, all applicants notified.
