# 22 — Management Scorecard

## PURPOSE
Give a weekly, numeric answer to "is the system working?" — separate from any single placement's story. Serves: **Money**, **Vacancy→Tenant** (aggregated).

## WHEN THIS SOP STARTS
Every week, and rolled up monthly for the CEO Dashboard sit-down.

## RESPONSIBLE PERSON
Leasing Lead compiles; Ebin reviews.

## ESTIMATED TIME
15 minutes weekly.

## REQUIRED INPUTS
- All active and recently-closed Campaign Closeout records
- Daily Leasing Routine completion log
- Owner Communication logs

---

## WEEKLY KPIs — **[CHECKLIST]** to compile, **[AUTOMATABLE]** wherever the underlying data already lives in the system

- [ ] Active vacancies (current count against the capacity limit of 2)
- [ ] Days to lease (median, across recently-closed placements)
- [ ] Median response time (first contact vs. inquiry time, from `09_LEAD_MANAGEMENT.md`)
- [ ] Inquiry → Showing % 
- [ ] Showing → Application %
- [ ] Application → Qualified %
- [ ] Qualified → Lease %
- [ ] Owner updates sent on time % (against the calendar in `16_OWNER_COMMUNICATION.md`)
- [ ] Overdue follow-ups (should be zero, per `21_QUALITY_CONTROL.md`)
- [ ] Vacancy loss (sum of daily vacancy cost, from `04_RENT_PRICING.md`'s vacancy cost calculator, across all currently-vacant days)
- [ ] Original asking rent vs. final rent (average variance across recent closeouts)
- [ ] Lead source performance (which channels are actually converting, not just generating volume)
- [ ] Top rejection reasons (aggregated across recent showings)

- [ ] Compile into the scorecard, week over week, so trend (not just snapshot) is visible — **[CHECKLIST]**

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Reading a single week's number in isolation instead of watching the trend.
- Treating "days to lease" as the only metric that matters — a fast lease at a rent 15% under market isn't actually a win; read it alongside the rent variance number.

## ESCALATION CONDITIONS
- Any conversion-rate KPI trending down for 3 consecutive weeks → run full funnel diagnostics across all active vacancies, not just the worst-performing one — this could be a systemic issue (e.g., a channel algorithm change) rather than a single-property problem.

## QUALITY CONTROL
- Scorecard compiled every week without gaps.

## OUTPUT / DELIVERABLE
A trended weekly scorecard, rolled into the monthly CEO Dashboard review.
