# 04 — Rent Pricing SOP

## PURPOSE
Turn "look at comparable listings" into a structured, repeatable process that produces a defensible number every time — even for properties with no direct comps. Serves: **Money**, **Vacancy→Tenant**.

## WHEN THIS SOP STARTS
After `03_OWNER_EXPECTATIONS.md`, before listing preparation begins, and again at any price-review checkpoint in `15_VACANCY_ESCALATION.md`.

## RESPONSIBLE PERSON
Leasing Lead.

## ESTIMATED TIME
30–45 minutes for a standard comp pull; up to 90 minutes for a no-direct-comps property.

## REQUIRED INPUTS
- Property address, type, bedrooms/bathrooms, size, condition, features (laundry, parking, utilities, AC, outdoor space, furnishing)
- Access to current rental listings in the area (Kijiji, Facebook Marketplace, Zillow/rental sites, local groups)
- Prospera's own listing/lease history for comparable units (Notion)

---

## THE 8-STEP COMPARABLE PROCESS

- [ ] **Step 1 — Direct comparables.** — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]** / **[AI ASSISTABLE]** for pulling listings
  Same building or, failing that, same street/block, same bedroom count, similar condition. WHO: Leasing Lead · DEFINITION OF DONE: 2+ direct comps found, or confirmed none exist → go to Step 2

- [ ] **Step 2 — Same building/complex.** If direct-block comps are thin, widen to the same building or complex, any unit type, and adjust for size/bedroom difference.

- [ ] **Step 3 — Same neighbourhood.** Widen to the neighbourhood boundary, same property type, similar condition and features.

- [ ] **Step 4 — Expand geography, preserve similarity.** Widen the radius but hold property type/condition/features roughly constant — don't let geography and features both drift at once.

- [ ] **Step 5 — Relax one variable at a time.** If still thin, relax exactly one variable (e.g., allow a slightly larger radius, OR one fewer bedroom, not both) and note which variable was relaxed.

- [ ] **Step 6 — Analyze substitute properties.** What else would this exact renter be looking at? A 2-bed apartment competes with a small 3-bed house in the same budget band — include those.

- [ ] **Step 7 — Analyze active competition.** Pull every currently-active listing in the direct competitive set. Note asking price, days on market if visible, and any price drops.

- [ ] **Step 8 — Adjust for meaningful differences.** Apply directional adjustments (not fake precision) for: bedrooms, bathrooms, property type, condition, size, laundry, parking, utilities included, AC, outdoor space, furnishing, location, amenities. State each adjustment as "+/- because X," not just a final number with no reasoning shown.

## NO-DIRECT-COMPS PRICING SOP (unique/luxury/rare property types)

When Step 1 through Step 4 turn up nothing usable:
- [ ] Widen geography further than normal, holding property type fixed — **[HUMAN JUDGMENT]**
- [ ] Identify substitute properties a renter with this budget/need would actually consider, even in a different category (e.g., furnished executive rental vs. short-term corporate housing)
- [ ] Compare feature-by-feature against the closest 2–3 substitutes found
- [ ] Check for revealed demand — has anything remotely similar rented recently, at what price, how fast?
- [ ] State confidence level explicitly as LOW when comps are this thin — never present a no-comps number with the same confidence as a 5-direct-comp number

## THREE PRICE POSITIONS

Present all three to the owner, every time — never just one number:

| Position | What it means | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| **Aggressive Price** | Top of the defensible range | Maximizes monthly rent if it works; higher risk of a longer vacancy |
| **Market Price** | Center of the defensible range | Balanced — the number most comps support |
| **Velocity Price** | Bottom of the defensible range | Minimizes vacancy time; leaves rent on the table if demand was actually stronger |

## OWNER RENT RECOMMENDATION SCRIPT
See `25_SCRIPT_LIBRARY.md`. Never present rent as guaranteed. Always state: **Estimated Market Range**, **Recommended Launch Price**, **Confidence Level** (High/Medium/Low based on comp quality), **Review Date** (when this will be revisited if the funnel isn't converting).

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Presenting one number as if it were fact instead of a range with a confidence level.
- Skipping straight to "similar listings" without checking active competition and revealed demand (Steps 6–7) — this is the step most likely to be skipped and most likely to matter.
- Letting the owner's desired number anchor the analysis instead of running the process first and comparing after.

## ESCALATION CONDITIONS
- Confidence level LOW on a property the owner is treating as urgent → flag to Ebin before presenting, consider extending the comp search radius further or waiting for one more data point.

## QUALITY CONTROL
- No rent recommendation goes to an owner without a documented comp set (even if thin) and a stated confidence level.

## OUTPUT / DELIVERABLE
A rent recommendation with Estimated Market Range, three price positions, confidence level, and review date — logged, not just spoken.
