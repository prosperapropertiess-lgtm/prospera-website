# 25 — Script Library

Every script referenced across this operating system, in one place, so nothing requires flipping between files during a live call or a fast-moving email thread. Written in first person, in Ebin's voice: calm, direct, professional, no filler. Adjust names/details before sending — these are starting points, not fill-in-the-blank forms.

---

## Property Acceptance (01)

**Property Decline Script**
> Thanks for walking me through the property — I want to be straight with you rather than take this on and not deliver. Based on [specific reason — location, condition, price gap], I don't think we're the right fit to place this one right now. [If applicable: if X changes, I'd be glad to take another look.] I'd rather tell you that now than after you've committed time waiting on us.

**Conditional Acceptance Script**
> I'd like to take this on, with a couple of things upfront so we're aligned: [specific condition, e.g., "the rent number we discussed is above what I'm seeing in the market right now — I'd want to launch at $X and revisit after two weeks of data" or "there's a repair item that needs to be handled before we can list"]. If that works for you, let's move forward.

---

## Capacity Management (02)

**Waitlist Script**
> I want to give this the attention it deserves, and right now my active placements are full. I can add you to the list and reach back out the moment I have capacity — realistically that's [timeframe]. I'd rather tell you that honestly than take this on and give it less than it needs.

**Owner Capacity Script** (explaining the limit if asked)
> I intentionally cap how many active vacancies I run at once. It's not about turning down business — it's so every property I'm actively marketing gets fast follow-up and real attention, not a slice of my time split six ways.

---

## Owner Expectations (03)

**Tenant Placement Expectations Script**
> Before we get started, I want to walk you through exactly how this works so there are no surprises. Here's what I handle: pricing advice, marketing, showings, screening, and communication with you at every step. Here's what I don't control: how the market responds, whether a specific renter falls in love with the place, and the final numbers — that's always your call, I just tell you what I'm seeing. On pricing — I'll give you a real range based on data, not a guess, and you decide where to launch. On timeline — I can't promise a date, but I promise you'll never have to wonder what's happening; I'll update you on a set schedule and act early if something's not working. On screening — I won't lower our standards to fill the vacancy faster. Sound good?

## Rent Pricing (04)

**Owner Rent Recommendation Script**
> Based on [X] comparable properties I pulled, here's what I'm seeing: an estimated market range of $[low]–$[high]. I'd recommend launching at $[recommended] — that's my confidence level [High/Medium/Low] based on the comp data available. I'll revisit this on [date] if we're not seeing the response I'd expect.

**Price Reduction Conversation Script**
> Here's the math: this property sits vacant, that's about $[X]/day in lost rent. Holding out for another $[Y]/month while we lose another month of vacancy costs more than the $[Y] gains back. I'm not saying drop the price blindly — I'm saying here's the tradeoff, and it's your call.

## Lead Management (09)

**New Inquiry Script**
> Hi [name], thanks for reaching out about [property]! It's still available. A couple quick questions so I can get you booked for a viewing: what's your target move-in date, and does the rent of $[X]/month work for your budget? [If website lead: skip re-asking what prequalification already answered.]

**Qualification Script**
> Great, sounds like a good fit on the basics. A couple property-specific things: [dealbreaker question relevant to this unit — e.g., laundry, parking]. Also — heads up, there's a $99 application fee if you move forward with an application; it goes toward your first month's rent if it works out. Still interested in seeing it?

**Viewing Booking Script**
> Perfect — I've got [day/time] open, does that work? I'll send a confirmation with the address and my contact info. Just bring yourself, nothing else needed for the viewing itself.

**No-Response Follow-Up (24h)**
> Hi [name], just following up on [property] — still want to grab a viewing time? Happy to answer any questions first if that's easier.

**Second Follow-Up (72h)**
> Circling back one more time on [property] in case this got buried — still available if you're interested. No pressure either way, just let me know.

**Final Follow-Up (7 days)**
> Last check-in on [property] — I'll assume the timing isn't right and won't follow up again, but reach out any time if that changes.

**Lost Lead Script** (when a lead self-identifies as no longer interested)
> Totally understand, thanks for letting me know. Mind sharing what didn't work — price, size, location, something else? Helps me point you toward something that might fit better if anything comes up.

**Price-Change Reactivation Script**
> Hi [name], quick update — the rent on [property] just changed to $[new price]. Since price was the main thing before, wanted to flag it in case it changes things on your end.

## Showings (10)

**Showing Script** (framework, not verbatim — adapt to the property)
> Walk through starting with the strongest room/feature, mention 2–3 real benefits specific to this unit, invite questions throughout rather than saving them to the end. Close with: "What questions can I answer, and how does this compare to what else you've seen?"

**No-Show Script**
> Hi [name], I was at [property] for our [time] viewing — didn't see you, hope everything's okay. Want to grab another time, or is this one off the table?

**Post-Showing Follow-Up — INTERESTED**
> Great meeting you today! Sounds like [property] could be a good fit. Ready to start an application, or do you have questions first?

**Post-Showing Follow-Up — MAYBE**
> Thanks for coming by. Totally get [specific objection] is a factor — [address it directly if there's a real answer, e.g., a pricing or feature clarification]. Let me know if that changes anything, or if there's something else on your mind.

**Post-Showing Follow-Up — NOT INTERESTED**
> Thanks for taking the time to see it, and for the honest feedback about [reason]. If anything changes or another property comes up that's a better fit, I'll keep you in mind.

## Applications (11)

**Application Started But Not Finished**
> Hey [name], noticed your application for [property] isn't quite finished — takes about 2 minutes to wrap up. Let me know if anything's unclear or isn't working.

**Documents Missing**
> Thanks for applying! To move forward I need [specific document(s) — be exact, not "your documents"]. You can send them by [method].

**Reference Outstanding**
> Hi [reference name], I'm processing a rental application for [applicant name] and they listed you as a reference. Would you have a few minutes for a couple quick questions about their tenancy?

**Employment Verification**
> Hi, I'm verifying employment for [applicant name] as part of a rental application. Could you confirm their position and current employment status?

**Application Withdrawn**
> No problem at all — thanks for letting me know, and good luck with the search.

**Application Status Update**
> Quick update on your application for [property]: [current stage — e.g., "we're finishing reference checks, expect a decision by [date]"]. Nothing needed from you right now.

## Screening (12)

**Landlord Reference Script**
> Consistent questions for every applicant: How long did they rent from you? Was rent paid on time? Any lease violations or complaints? Did they give proper notice at move-out? Would you rent to them again?

## Owner Communication (16)

**Launch Update**
> [Property] went live today on [channels]. Launch price: $[X]. I'll check in with a full update on [Day 7 date].

**Day 7 Update**
> One week in on [property]. Numbers: [views/inquiries/showings]. Feedback: [top themes if any]. Actions taken: [what was done this week]. Assessment: [on track / needs a look]. Recommendation: [none needed / specific suggestion]. Next update: [date].

**Day 14 Update**
> Two weeks in. [Same structure as Day 7, with the added pricing check from `04_RENT_PRICING.md` if funnel data suggests it.]

**Price Recommendation**
See Owner Rent Recommendation Script above.

**Showing Feedback Update**
> Had a showing today — [outcome]. [If a repeated objection is emerging across multiple showings, name the pattern, not just this one instance.]

**Strong Applicant Update**
> Good news — [name] applied for [property] and looks like a strong candidate on paper. Starting verification now, should have a full picture by [date].

**Application Issue Update**
> Wanted to flag something on [name]'s application before we go further: [specific issue]. Here's how I'm handling it: [action].

**Owner Decision Request**
> I need a decision from you on [specific thing] by [date] so we can keep moving. Here's the situation: [context]. Here's what I'd recommend: [recommendation, if applicable].

**Lease Signed Update**
> [Property] is leased. [Tenant name], starting [date], at $[final rent]. Full closeout summary to follow.

**Difficult Market Update**
> Wanted to give you a direct read on the market for [property] right now: [specific data — comparable inventory, days-on-market trend]. This is affecting demand broadly, not just this listing. Here's what I'm adjusting: [action].

**No Qualified Demand Update**
> We've had [X] showings on [property] but haven't seen a qualified application yet. Here's what the feedback is telling me: [pattern]. Here's what I want to try next: [action].

**30-Day Vacancy Review**
> [Property] has been active for 30 days. Full picture: [funnel numbers, everything tried]. Here's where I think we go from here: [2–3 concrete options]. Let's find 15 minutes to decide together.
