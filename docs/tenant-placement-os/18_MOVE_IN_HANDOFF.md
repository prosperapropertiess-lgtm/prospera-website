# 18 — Leasing → Move-In Handoff

## PURPOSE
Transfer everything about this tenancy cleanly so nothing downstream (maintenance, future renewals, owner questions) depends on anyone's memory. Serves: **Move-In/Move-Out**, **Owner Communication**.

## WHEN THIS SOP STARTS
Immediately after `17_LEASE_EXECUTION.md` is complete.

## RESPONSIBLE PERSON
Leasing Lead (handing off to whoever manages ongoing tenant relations — Ebin directly for placement-only clients, or Prospera's management track if this property is also under full management).

## ESTIMATED TIME
20–30 minutes.

## REQUIRED INPUTS
- Signed lease
- Full tenant record from application/screening

---

## HANDOFF CHECKLIST

- [ ] Tenant information transferred — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Full contact info, lease terms, household details as disclosed.

- [ ] Lease document transferred — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Copy accessible to whoever needs it going forward.

- [ ] Payment/deposit records transferred — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Deposit amount, method, receipt reference.

- [ ] Move-in date confirmed and transferred — **[CHECKLIST]**

- [ ] Keys/access transferred — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Confirm exactly what was given to the tenant and what remains with the owner/Prospera.

- [ ] Utilities and parking details transferred — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Matches what was disclosed in the listing and lease.

- [ ] Special terms transferred — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Any negotiated terms outside the standard lease (pet agreement, parking assignment, etc.).

- [ ] Maintenance items transferred — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Anything flagged IMPORTANT (not BLOCKER) in `06_RENT_READY.md` that's still outstanding — this is the direct link to Prospera's Maintenance pillar/module.

- [ ] Inspection requirements transferred — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Move-in condition inspection scheduled/completed, documented with photos/notes.

- [ ] Contact information confirmed both directions — **[CHECKLIST]**
  Tenant knows who to contact for what; owner knows the tenant's status is now "leased," not "vacancy."

## COMMON MISTAKES
- Treating the handoff as informal ("they'll figure it out") instead of an actual checklist — this is exactly the kind of memory-dependency the whole system exists to eliminate.
- Forgetting to transfer IMPORTANT-but-not-BLOCKER maintenance items, so they silently vanish instead of becoming the new tenant's first maintenance request.
- Skipping the move-in condition inspection — this protects both the owner and the tenant at move-out time.

## ESCALATION CONDITIONS
- Any handoff item genuinely can't be transferred (missing information) → resolve before calling the handoff complete, don't let a gap carry forward silently.

## QUALITY CONTROL
- Handoff checklist 100% complete before this vacancy is marked LEASED/closed in `19_CAMPAIGN_CLOSEOUT.md`.

## OUTPUT / DELIVERABLE
A complete, transferred tenancy record — nothing about this tenancy depends on the Leasing Lead's memory going forward.
